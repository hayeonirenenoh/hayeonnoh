About

How legal and institutional systems respond to gender-based violence, and how feminist research can make them work for survivors.

Hayeon Noh is a final-year student at Sogang University, double-majoring in Global Korean Studies and Women's Studies (GPA 4.15 / 4.3). Her interest in gender-based violence began in middle school, when she saw how little institutions did in response to sexual violence.

She works across research and civil society. She has planned a campaign for a consent-based rape law with the Korea Sexual Violence Relief Center, analysed gender bias in generative AI for the Seoul Foundation of Women and Family, and interviewed women cleaning workers and subcontracted shipyard workers. She studied political science in Madrid and works in Korean, English and Spanish.
